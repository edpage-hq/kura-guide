Getting started ​

What applies to every account, whatever your roles.

Create your account ​

There is no sign-up form for Kura: the account is born from your first step on the NextBoxx website, for example submitting a candidature or registering for an event. If your e-mail address already has an account, it is reused.

For a new account, the acknowledgement e-mail contains a link to choose your password. The link is valid for 7 days and works only once. If it has expired, use "Forgot your password?" on the login page.

Sign in ​

Go to the Log in page and enter your e-mail and password. "Remember me" keeps the session open on this device.

If you have added a passkey, you may prefer it: "Sign in with a passkey" saves you typing a password. If you have enabled two-factor authentication, Kura then asks for the code from your app, or one of your recovery codes.

An account inactive for a year is deactivated, its data kept. It is reactivated as soon as you sign in again or submit a new candidature.

Secure your account ​

In Settings → Security:

  • change your password;
  • enable two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. Keep the recovery codes shown on activation: they are useful if you lose your phone;
  • add one or more passkeys (for example the one on your computer or phone), and name them so you can recognise them.

Your profile ​

In Settings → Profile: your name, e-mail address, phone and photo. Enter your phone in international format (for example +228 90 00 00 00): this is the number to which Kura can send payment and admission reminders on WhatsApp. If you change your e-mail address, a verification link is sent to you.

Settings → Language chooses French or English, Settings → Appearance the light theme, the dark theme or your system's.

Ask for a role ​

Settings → Roles shows your active roles and the history of your requests.

To ask for another one, choose it in "Ask for a role", add a few words of introduction if you wish and send. The request is examined by the person responsible for that role; you are told by e-mail of the decision, with a reason in case of refusal. While you wait, your other roles stay active, and a refusal does not change them.

Staff roles (Coordinator, Advisor, Direction, etc.) cannot be requested: the Administrator assigns them. The Candidate, Participant and Learner roles arrive automatically with the corresponding step.

Individual or organization account ​

An account is either an individual or an organization. A company wishing to fund learners, order a training on quote or look for profiles needs an organization account. It is not created separately: you convert your individual account.

In Settings → Roles, under "Organization account", enter the company name, attach a supporting document (company registration or equivalent, PDF, JPG or PNG, 5 MB at most) and send. The Administrator examines the request. On approval, the account takes the company name as its name, and its history stays readable, read-only.

An organization account can no longer apply or register for an event personally.

My documents ​

My documents (Journeys section) gathers everything Kura has produced for your account, each with its reference and date:

  • training agreements, tripartite funding agreements and internship agreements;
  • the welcome booklet;
  • invoices and credit notes;
  • certificate, partial attestation and transcript of competencies;
  • decision letters of your candidatures.

A filter by document type helps find the right one. Each document opens or downloads as a PDF.

Notifications ​

Kura writes to you to keep you informed: the e-mail is always sent. If your Sève account is open, the message also arrives in Sève; if you have entered a phone, some reminders (payments, admission) are also sent on WhatsApp. A channel that fails does not stop the others.
