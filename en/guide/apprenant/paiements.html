Payments ​

The price of the training is paid in three instalments, set by your agreement. You follow invoices and due dates in Journeys → My enrolment, under "Invoices".

The 30 / 40 / 30 schedule ​

InstalmentShare of the priceDue date
Signature30%At the start of the cohort, or at signing if later
Mid-point40%In the middle of the cohort
Balance30%At the end of the cohort

The exact dates are in your agreement. Amounts are excluding tax: the invoice carries the exemption mention and the payment details.

Invoices ​

Each instalment gives an invoice (reference FAC-NB-YYYY-XXX), sent as a PDF by e-mail and available in My documents:

  • the first is issued at the counter-signature of your agreement;
  • the next two are issued 15 days before their due date.

If a company funds you in part, each instalment is invoiced in two separate invoices: yours for your share, theirs for theirs. Each is followed separately.

How to pay ​

Payments are received outside Kura, then recorded by the Pedagogical Coordinator with the amount, method, reference and date. You can pay by bank transfer, cheque, Mobile Money (Flooz, T-Money), card or cash. The precise payment details are on each invoice.

A partial payment is possible; the recorded amount can never exceed what is due. For each payment recorded, you receive an acknowledgement.

Reminders ​

Kura warns you before and after each due date, by e-mail, and also in Sève and on WhatsApp if your account is open and your phone entered:

  • 15, 7 and 1 days before the due date;
  • the day after the due date, if the instalment is unpaid.

If an instalment is not paid ​

The timeline is the same for every instalment:

WhenWhat happens
Due dateThe instalment must be paid
D+1Automatic reminder
D+7The Coordinator contacts you (e-mail or phone)
D+15The invoice becomes "Overdue". A 15-day moratorium starts, automatic and unique. You are notified
D+30Without payment, your access to Sève is suspended. You are notified
After D+30Interview with the Coordinator, possible repayment plan; unenrolment if there is no settlement

Paying, at any moment, restores your access automatically. The suspension only concerns Sève: your documents and your Kura file remain accessible.

If the funder is late, your access is suspended too, even if your own share is paid.

If your situation changes ​

  • Postponement or force majeure. You change cohort: what you have paid is kept. What remained due on invoices already issued is cancelled by a credit note (AV-NB-YYYY-XXX), and the new agreement re-invoices each instalment, deducting what you have already paid.
  • Cohort cancelled by NextBoxx before it starts: Kura issues a credit note for a 100% refund of the sums paid. The Coordinator records the refund once it is made.
  • Drop-out. No credit note: instalments not yet invoiced will no longer be, but invoices already issued remain due. You receive a partial attestation for the validated blocks (see Certificate and Canopé).

The balance conditions the certificate ​

The balance paid in full is one of the four conditions of the certificate (see Certificate and Canopé).
