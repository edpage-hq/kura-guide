# Direction and reporting

Two pages of the **Overview** section give the overall view of activity, **read-only**: the **Direction view** and **Reporting**. They rest on the same periods, the same filters and the same definitions.

| Page               | For whom                               | Particularity                                               |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Direction view** | Direction, Administrator               | Shows what was paid out and the margin                      |
| **Reporting**      | Pedagogical Coordinator, Administrator | No payout or margin; with attendance and grades; CSV export |

## Choosing what you look at

Both pages cover **cohorts that start in the chosen year** (the current year by default). Then filter by **program** and by **promotion**. A postponed or cancelled cohort counts neither in places nor in zones.

## The Direction view

**Training:**

- cohorts **by status**;
- **headcount zones** (Fragile, Viable, Comfortable), frozen at the start for started cohorts, current for upcoming cohorts;
- the **fill rate**: learners over places.

**Success rate.** It covers **completed** cohorts: certified learners over learners who finished. A learner "who finished" is one who completed a finished cohort, certified or not; **a drop-out after signing counts as a failure**.

**Finance:**

| Indicator        | Definition                                |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Invoiced         | Net of cancellation credit notes          |
| Collected        | Net of refunds                            |
| Still to collect | Including the overdue amount              |
| Paid out         | Trainers', coaches' and jury members' pay |
| Gross margin     | Collected minus paid out                  |

A detail **by program** and **by promotion** accompanies these figures.

**L'Éclosion participations.** The portfolio lists participations validated by the investment committee, all cycles together: project, cycle, type, share and validation date.

## Reporting

Reporting repeats fill, success (with the number of partial attestations), invoiced, collected and outstanding, and adds:

- **attendance**: the time present of confirmed learners, present or late, over the length of the sessions recorded for them;
- the **overall average**: the average of the weighted averages of learners with at least one graded block, out of 20.

The detail is **by program**, **by promotion**, then **one line per cohort**, which leads to the cohort's page.

**Export as CSV** produces the list of the selection's cohorts (semicolon separator, UTF-8, in the user's language).

## The investment committee

The Direction also sits on L'Éclosion's **investment committee**, with the Incubation Officer and the Pedagogical Coordinator: see [L'Éclosion](../activites/eclosion#the-participation-and-the-investment-committee). It is the only place where the Direction acts, the rest of its pages being read-only.
