# Recruiting certified profiles

NextBoxx connects companies with its **certified learners** who wish it, for an internship, a work-study placement, an assignment or a recruitment.

## Submitting a request

In **Journeys → My company requests → New request**, choose the type:

| Type            | Specific fields                                                                                           |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Placement**   | Internship, work-study or assignment; skills sought; wished start; length in months; location             |
| **Recruitment** | Job title; contract (permanent or fixed-term); location; indicative salary (optional); wished hiring date |

In both cases, also give the company name, the **number of profiles sought** (1 to 20), a target program (optional), a message, and whether you want **support**: NextBoxx then follows the integration of each retained profile, for an additional lump sum per profile.

The "My company requests" entry is visible to every personal account, even before the first request. The Partnerships Officer is notified of your request.

## The proposed profiles

The Partnerships Officer proposes to you, in one or several goes, **certified and visible** profiles: learners who have chosen to make themselves visible to companies. For each, you first see their **first name and the initial of their last name**, their **certificates** (program, mention, date) and their **presentation**.

For each profile, you choose **Retain** or **Decline** (the Officer can also record it for you).

- A **retained** profile reveals to you the person's **name, e-mail address, phone and CV**, and they are told of it.
- A profile that withdraws (because the person hides themselves) leaves the pending proposals; a profile already retained stays shared.

## Confirmation and invoice

As soon as at least one profile is retained, the Officer confirms the **placement**. An invoice is then issued to your company name, **due in 30 days**. It counts, **per retained profile**:

- the placement lump sum;
- the support lump sum, if you asked for it.

The request becomes "Accepted" and profiles still pending are closed as not retained. If no profile fits, the Officer closes the request, with no invoice.

Reminders and follow-up of this invoice follow the usual timeline, **with no access suspension**.
