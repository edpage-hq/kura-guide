# Funding learners

A company or organization can take on all or part of a learner's training. The **Funder** role gives it a dedicated space, **Journeys → Funded learners**.

## Getting the Funder role

The role requires an **organization account**: if your account is an individual account, [convert it first](../premiers-pas#individual-or-organization-account). Then ask for the **Funder** role in **Settings → Roles**. The request is examined by the Partnerships Officer, who tells you of their decision by e-mail.

## How a funding starts

The funding is decided **with the learner, before their agreement is signed**. The Pedagogical Coordinator designates your account as funder and sets your share, from 1 to 100%.

From then on:

- the **split** appears in the training agreement (for example 60% for the learner, 40% for you);
- each instalment is invoiced in **two separate invoices**, one per payer, each followed separately;
- if you fund 100%, the learner has nothing to pay.

## Your "Funded learners" space

You see there **only the learners you fund**: their training, their cohort, their status and **your share**, as well as the **invoices for your share**. You see neither the other funders nor the learner's invoices.

Invoices are also sent to you as a PDF by e-mail, and filed in **My documents**.

## Signing the tripartite agreement

When the learner's agreement is counter-signed by edPage group, a **tripartite funding agreement** is prepared for you. It repeats the terms of the agreement: the parties, the training, the cohort, the price, your share and the schedule.

To sign it, from **Funded learners**:

1. open the learner's tripartite agreement;
2. scroll to the end and tick "I have read the agreement to the end and accept its terms";
3. ask for the code: a **6-digit** code is sent to your e-mail address, valid **15 minutes**, usable once, with 5 tries;
4. enter it and sign.

edPage group then counter-signs. The sealed PDF (`CONVT-NB-YYYY-XXX`) is sent to you and to the learner.

This agreement **delays neither the enrolment nor billing**: they already rest on the learner's agreement. If the learner changes cohort, the tripartite is replaced along with their agreement, and a new one is offered to you.

## Late payments

Reminders and the timeline of a non-payment are those of any invoice: reminders 15, 7 and 1 days before the due date, a reminder the next day, the Coordinator's follow-up at D+7, overdue and 15-day moratorium at D+15, suspension at D+30.

**If your share is late, the learner's access to Sève is suspended**, even if they have paid theirs. It is restored as soon as you pay.

The training balance must be paid, by the learner **and** by you, for the certificate to be issued.
