# Lead Trainer

The Lead Trainer teaches and grades in **Sève**, and handles in **Kura** what concerns their cohorts: in-person attendance, retakes, remuneration tracking. The role is assigned by the Pedagogical Coordinator.

## Your cohorts

**Training → Cohorts** lists only **the cohorts you are assigned to**. Opening another trainer's cohort is refused. Each page shows its dates, headcount, learners with their format and status, and the cohort's documents (see below).

In the learner list, a **"No image rights"** marker flags those who refused: do not share any recording in which they appear.

You cannot create, confirm, postpone or cancel a cohort: that is the Coordinator's business. Nor do you have access to the admission test's question bank.

## Attendance

From the cohort page, **Attendance** gives, for each learner, the absence rate (missed hours over the training's hours) with a marker at the 30% threshold, and the list of classes.

- **Virtual classes** are read from Sève every hour. They appear here without you entering anything, and cannot be edited in Kura. For this, the Coordinator must have entered the cohort's Sève course id.
- **In-person classes** you enter yourself: **Record a class**, with the date and time, the length in minutes and an optional title.

Entry is only possible **once the cohort has started**, and the class must have taken place. Each in-person or hybrid learner is **present by default**: untick those who are absent, or mark them late or absent. A late arrival counts as present, an excused absence as absent.

You can **correct** or **delete** an in-person class; rates are recalculated, and everything is logged. When a learner's rate exceeds 30%, they are notified, as is the pedagogical team, once only. A correction bringing it back under the threshold rearms the alert.

## Grades in Sève

You grade each **skill block** in the Sève course gradebook, out of 20. For Kura to find the grade, the grade item carries the block's code as its **ID number**: `KURA-BLOC-{id}`. The codes of all blocks are listed on the cohort's **Results** page.

Grades flow back into Kura every hour. A new grade on an already graded block **corrects** the current attempt.

## Results and retakes

The cohort's **Results** page shows, for each learner, their grades by block, their average (weighted by block duration) and the jury's decision. A block is:

| Grade              | Reading                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| 10/20 and more     | Validated                           |
| From 8 to under 10 | Compensable: made up by the average |
| Under 8            | Eliminatory                         |

You can **open a retake** for a block under 10/20, **at least 15 days** after the block's last grade, within **2 retakes** (3 attempts in all). The page shows the date from which it is possible.

An open retake **waits for the first grade given in Sève after it opens** and fills itself. You can cancel it as long as it has not been graded.

The **jury's** decisions (tutored project, defence, internship) are entered by the Coordinator. Issuing the certificate is also up to them.

## The cohort's documents

From the cohort page, you download, generated on demand:

- the **provisional calendar**: blocks, key dates, payment due dates, jury days, public holidays;
- the **defence report**, once produced by the Coordinator.

The activity report (seats, attendance, results) stays reserved for the Coordinator and the Administrator.

## Your remuneration

**Finance → My remuneration** shows your lines **cohort by cohort** and the payouts received, with three totals: awaiting validation, validated to pay, paid.

Kura calculates your remuneration **at the end of the cohort**, at the rates in force at its start. The Coordinator adjusts it if needed and validates it; payment is made outside Kura and they record it. If there are several trainers on a cohort, your share is split equally.
