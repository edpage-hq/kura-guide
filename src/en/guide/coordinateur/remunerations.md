# Remunerations

Sums due to trainers, coaches, jury members are prepared in **Finance**. Kura calculates or records, **you validate**, then you **record the payment made outside Kura**: Kura executes no transfer.

## Trainers, coaches and jury

**Finance → Remunerations** lists each payee's lines, by cohort and by kind: teaching hours, project lump sum, coaching, jury token.

### The pay grid

From the page, the **pay grid** gives the rates in force: hourly rate by profitability zone, project lump sum per cohort, coaching lump sum per learner at the start, jury token per member and per defence date. **Schedule a rate** makes it take effect on a date. A cohort keeps the rates in force **at its start**.

### Calculating

At the end of a cohort, Kura calculates:

- **teaching hours**: the program's hours × the **zone's rate at the start**;
- the **project lump sum**;
- **coaching**: headcount at the start × lump sum per learner.

If several trainers (or several coaches) share the cohort, the sum is **split equally**. The headcount and zone used are those of the **start**, even if a learner drops out afterwards.

If a rate was missing at the close, the cohort appears under **Completed cohorts to calculate** with the missing rates: complete the grid, then **Calculate**.

**Jury tokens** are due to each recorded member (see [Results and certification](./resultats#the-jury-members)) once the defence date has passed.

### Adjusting, validating, paying

1. **Adjust** a line if needed: the calculated amount stays visible, the adjustment is logged.
2. Select lines and **validate** them.
3. When the payment is made, **record it**: the validated lines are settled.

Each payee follows their lines in **My remuneration**.

Commissions for business referrers and marketing partners are no longer managed in Kura: they are tracked in Naia.
