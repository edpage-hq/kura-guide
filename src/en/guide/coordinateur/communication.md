# Communication

**Training → Communication** has two sections: **Announcements** and **Deliveries**. The Pedagogical Coordinator and the Administrator have access. Day-to-day exchanges between learners and trainers stay in Sève.

## Sending an announcement

**New announcement**:

1. Choose the **recipients**: **A cohort** (current or upcoming) or **A program**, which targets all its current or upcoming cohorts.
2. Tick or untick **Include the assigned trainers and coach-tutors**, ticked by default.
3. Enter the **subject** and the **message** (separate paragraphs with a blank line).
4. **Send the announcement**.

Sending is **immediate**, by e-mail, and on Sève for accounts open there. There is **no WhatsApp** for announcements.

Recipients are learners whose enrolment is pending signature, active or suspended. Each is served **only once**, even if in several targeted cohorts; an announcement **with no recipient is refused**.

The **Announcements sent** list keeps, for each, its author, number of recipients, e-mails, Sève deliveries and failures.

## The deliveries log

**Deliveries** shows **everything the notification engine has sent**: reminders, notices and announcements, channel by channel, with the recipient, message, date, status (**Sent** or **Failed**) and any error.

Filter by channel and status, search for a recipient or subject, and use **Show failures** to find what did not get through, with the number of failures over the last 30 days. A failing channel does not stop the others: a message that could not go out on Sève went out by e-mail.
