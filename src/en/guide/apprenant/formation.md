# Following your training

Once your first instalment is paid, you move from administration to the training itself, which takes place in **Sève**. Kura keeps tracking your attendance and results.

## Opening your access to Sève

Your Sève account opens **automatically when the first instalment is paid**. If a company funds part of your training, **both shares** of this first instalment must be paid.

You are enrolled in your cohort's course and you receive your **welcome booklet** (`LA-NB-YYYY-XXX`): by e-mail, then in **My enrolment** and **My documents**. It presents your training and, by name, the cohort's team with their e-mail addresses. A change of cohort produces a new one; the old one is kept.

To sign in to Sève, use your **NextBoxx account**: Sève relies on Kura to identify you, there is no second password to remember.

## Attendance

- **Virtual classes.** Your attendance is read in Sève and flows back into Kura every hour. It cannot be edited in Kura; in case of discrepancy, the virtual class log prevails.
- **In-person classes.** The cohort's Lead Trainer records the attendance of each in-person or hybrid learner after the session. They can correct it afterwards.

A **late arrival counts as present**; an **excused absence counts as absent**.

Your absence rate is the time of missed sessions relative to the training's hours (500 h, 700 h for Web & Mobile Development, or 250 h). When it exceeds **30%**, you are notified, as are the Pedagogical Coordinators, once only: a correction bringing you back under the threshold rearms the alert.

## Grades and blocks

The training is divided into **skill blocks**, each graded out of 20 in Sève. Grades flow back into Kura every hour.

| Block grade        | Effect                                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| 10/20 or more      | Block validated                               |
| From 8 to under 10 | Not validated, but compensable by the average |
| Under 8            | Eliminatory                                   |

Your **average** is weighted by the duration of each block, in weeks. It is recalculated at each reading. If a block was attempted several times, the grade kept is that of the last graded attempt.

### The retake

For a block under 10/20, a Lead Trainer of your cohort or the Coordinator can open a **retake**:

- at most **2 retakes** per block (3 attempts in all);
- **at least 15 days** after your last grade on that block;
- it is filled with the first grade given in Sève after it opens, and can be cancelled as long as it has not been graded.

## The tutored project, the defence and the internship

Besides the blocks, the Coordinator records the **jury's** decisions:

- the **tutored project**: its subject, whether it is validated, its grade;
- the **defence**: its date, whether it is validated, its grade;
- the **internship**: the company, and whether the internship is validated.

Jury grades stay **outside the average**. A validated defence is a condition of the certificate; the internship does not block the certificate. When the jury has decided on every defence, a **report** (`PVS-NB-YYYY-XXX`) is produced, numbered and sealed.

## The internship agreement

If your training includes an internship, the Coordinator enters the information in Kura: the host company and its signing contact, the cohort Coach-Tutor who supervises the internship, the dates and the mission. They then send the **internship agreement** to sign. As long as nobody has signed, they can edit it; an edit cancels the sending.

- **You**, an adult learner, sign it in your account with a code received by e-mail. A minor goes through their legal guardian, with a link valid for 7 days.
- **The company** signs through a link valid for 7 days and a code, without an account.
- edPage group then counter-signs. The sealed PDF (`CONVS-NB-YYYY-XXX`) is sent to you, to the Coach-Tutor, to the company and to your legal guardian if there is one.

The internship is part of the training and gives rise to no employment contract.

## Your tracking documents

Everything produced for you (booklet, invoices, agreements, transcript of competencies) is in **My documents**.
