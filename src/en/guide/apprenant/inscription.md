# Enrolment and agreement

When the decision is "admitted", Kura creates your **enrolment**. It stays "pending signature" until you have signed your training agreement. Everything happens in **Journeys → My enrolment**.

## Complete your file

Before you can sign, three things must be in place. The **My enrolment** page lists what is missing.

### A cohort

Your **cohort** is the session you will follow, with its start and end dates. It is chosen by the advisor at admission, or otherwise by the Pedagogical Coordinator, who tells you by e-mail. You do not choose it yourself.

### A format

You confirm or change your **format** (online, hybrid, in person) among those the cohort offers, with their price. You can change it **until the agreement is signed**; it is then fixed.

### Two consents

Answer each **separately** ("I agree" or "I do not agree"):

- **Teaching reuse of the tutored project**: NextBoxx may reuse, for teaching purposes, the project you carry out during the training;
- **Image rights**: NextBoxx may show you in its recordings.

Both answers are required to sign, but nothing obliges you to agree. You can change your mind **at any time**; each change is recorded. A refusal is respected everywhere: the team sees in the cohort page which learners have not given their image rights and shares no recording in which you appear.

## Sign the agreement

When the file is complete, Kura sends you a message and the **My enrolment** page shows "Your agreement is ready".

1. Click **Read the agreement**. It includes the general terms, then the specific terms: the parties, the training, the cohort, the format, the **price excluding tax** and the **schedule** of the three payments with their dates.
2. Scroll to the end: the box "I have read the agreement to the end and accept its terms" only becomes active then.
3. Click **Get a code**. A **6-digit** code is sent to your e-mail address. It is valid for **15 minutes**, works only once, and you have 5 tries.
4. Enter the code and click **Sign the agreement**.

Your agreement is then "awaiting counter-signature": the pedagogical team counter-signs for edPage group (automatically or by hand, depending on the chosen setting). You then receive the sealed PDF by e-mail; it is also in **My documents**, under the reference `CONV-NB-YYYY-XXX`.

On counter-signature, your enrolment becomes **active**, and the **format, cohort and price are fixed**. The first invoice is issued (see [Payments](./paiements)).

## If you are a minor

For a minor learner, the **legal guardian** gives consent and signs, without needing a Kura account:

1. In **My enrolment**, under "Legal guardian", enter their full name and e-mail address, then **Send the link**.
2. They receive a link to give the two consents. The link is valid for **7 days** and works only once; **Send a new link** replaces the previous one.
3. When the file is complete, you send them the agreement with **Send the agreement to my guardian**. They read it, receive a 6-digit code at their own address and sign from a second link, valid 7 days.

Only the legal guardian's consents and signature count.

## When a company funds you

If a company funds part of your training, the Coordinator records it **before the signature**. The agreement states the split (for example 60% learner, 40% funder) and each instalment is invoiced to each for their share.

A **tripartite agreement** is also prepared when your agreement is counter-signed. It repeats the same terms; the funder signs it, from their "Funded learners" space. It blocks neither your enrolment nor billing. You receive its PDF once both parties have signed.

## If your cohort changes

Any change of cohort after signing (postponement decided by NextBoxx, force majeure) **replaces the agreement**:

- the old one is kept, marked "replaced";
- your enrolment goes back to pending signature;
- a new agreement, **at the same price and in the same format**, is offered to you. You are told by e-mail.

What you have already paid is kept and deducted from the instalments of the new agreement.
