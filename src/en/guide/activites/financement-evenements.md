# Funding an event

A funder can support a NextBoxx community event with a **contribution**, in money or in kind. The **Events Officer** records receipt of contributions and keeps the expenses.

## Becoming an Event Funder

The **Event funder** role stays open to **individual accounts** as well as organization accounts. Ask for it in **Settings → Roles**: the Events Officer examines it. It is also assigned to you automatically when the Officer records a contribution in your name.

## Declaring a contribution

In **Activities → My funding**, **Declare a contribution** asks for:

- the **event**: it must be published, not cancelled and not yet over;
- the **kind**: financial, in kind or other;
- a **description**;
- the **amount** in FCFA, or, for an in-kind or other contribution, its **valuation** in FCFA.

Your contribution stays **Declared** until NextBoxx **records its receipt**, or **refuses** it with a reason that you receive by e-mail. It is never counted automatically, and no invoice is issued.

When the contribution is received, NextBoxx sends you your **funding agreement** (`CONVE-NB-YYYY-XXX`) by e-mail. It is not signed electronically.

## What you see

**My funding** lists your contributions, with their status (**Declared**, **Received**, **Refused**, **Cancelled**) and their agreement. For each event you fund, you see the **total contributions received**, the **total expenses** and the **net balance**. You see neither the detail of other funders, nor that of expenses.

If the event is **cancelled**, your declared contributions are cancelled. Contributions already received stay in the balance and you are notified; their return is settled outside Kura.

## Follow-up by the Events Officer

On an event's page, the **Funding** section shows contributions received, expenses and net balance, with the list of contributions.

- **Record receipt** of a declared contribution, with the date: it enters the balance and its funding agreement is issued and sent.
- **Refuse** a contribution, with a reason given to the funder.
- **Record a contribution** for an existing account, for example after the fact, even after the event ends. Enter the account's e-mail address; if you fill in "Already received on", receipt is recorded at once. The account receives the Event funder role.
- **Add an expense**: label, amount, date and optional receipt (PDF, JPG or PNG, 5 MB at most). You can correct or delete it; each entry, correction or deletion is traced in the log.
- **Note the return** of a contribution received after the event is cancelled, with its date: the refund is settled outside Kura and the contribution stays in the balance.

The **net balance** is the total contributions received minus expenses.
