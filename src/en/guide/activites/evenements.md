# Events

NextBoxx organizes events, free or paid. Any personal account can sign up for them; the **Events Officer** prepares and follows them.

## Signing up for an event

**Activities → My events** lists upcoming events and your sign-ups. You can also sign up from the NextBoxx website: your account is then created, or reused, and the **Participant** role is added to your roles. This role gives no access to a training.

Click **Sign up**. The status of your sign-up tells you where you stand:

| Status               | What it means                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Confirmed**        | Your place is confirmed                                                                                          |
| **Awaiting payment** | Your place is reserved until the payment deadline. Without payment, the sign-up is cancelled and the place freed |
| **Waiting list**     | The event is full; you will be notified as soon as a place frees up                                              |
| **Cancelled**        | Cancelled by you, for lack of payment in time, or because the event is cancelled                                 |

A few rules:

- **A free event** confirms your sign-up at once, and accepts registrants until it starts.
- **A paid event** issues an invoice at sign-up, **to be paid before the deadline**, 48 hours before the start. Full payment confirms the sign-up. Without full payment by that date, the sign-up is cancelled, the invoice cancelled by a credit note, and the place freed. Payment details are in "Payment details".
- **A freed place** (or a raised capacity) goes to the first on the **waiting list**, who is notified, and who receives their invoice if the event is paid.
- Sign-ups are refused if the event is closed or if you are already signed up. A **suspended account** for non-payment cannot sign up for a **paid** event (a free event remains possible). An **organization account** does not sign up: each participant does so with their personal account.

### Cancelling your sign-up

**Cancel my sign-up** frees your place for the next person and cancels an unpaid invoice. You can cancel from the waiting list, while awaiting payment, or a confirmed sign-up on a **free** event. **A place already paid cannot be cancelled** from your space: contact the team.

If you sign up again afterwards, your cancelled sign-up is taken up again with a new invoice.

## Organizing events

The Events Officer and the Administrator manage **Activities → Events**. The list is organized into **Upcoming**, **Past** and **Cancelled**.

### Creating and publishing

**New event**: title, presentation (shown on the NextBoxx website and in Kura), start, end (optional), venue, **places** and **price** in FCFA (0 for free). It is created as a **draft** and takes sign-ups **only once published**.

- **Publish** shows it on the website and in Kura.
- Dates are in the future and the end after the start.
- As soon as there is a registrant, **the price no longer changes** and the capacity cannot fall below the places taken. Raising the capacity gives its places to the waiting list.
- Registrants are **not notified** of modifications.

### Following the registrants

An event's page shows places taken, the waiting list, the payment deadline, and each registrant with their status, sign-up date and **invoice** (with the balance due). The Officer consults invoices; **the Coordinator records payments** (see [Billing](../coordinateur/facturation)). Event invoices enter neither the billing calendar, nor follow-ups, nor the Direction and Reporting dashboards.

### Cancelling an event

**Cancel the event** is possible as long as it has not started, with a **reason given to the registrants**. Each registrant is cancelled and notified; what was paid is refunded by a **credit note**, what remained due is cancelled by a credit note.
