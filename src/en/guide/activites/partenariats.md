# Technology partnerships

A technology partnership integrates an organization's tool, platform or certification into one or more programs. It is **free**, in exchange for **visibility** to learners.

The **Partnerships Officer** and the Administrator manage them, in **Activities → Technology partnerships**.

## Creating a partnership

**New partnership**: the partnership is born **Under negotiation**. Enter:

- the **partner organisation** and the **solution name**;
- the **nature**: pedagogical integration, certification or tool supply;
- the **programs concerned** (at least one);
- the partner's **signatory**, with their name and e-mail;
- the **planned end**, optional.

A partnership can be edited, or deleted, **until it is signed**. Editing a partnership whose contract is already sent withdraws that sending: the contract must be sent again.

## Getting the contract signed

**Send the contract** sends the signatory a link by e-mail, valid **7 days**. They read the contract to the end, accept it on behalf of their organization and sign it with a **code** received at the same address, **with no Kura account**. **Send the contract again** issues a new link.

Then edPage group **counter-signs**: automatically (by default) or, depending on the Administrator's setting, by a Partnerships Officer with **Counter-sign**.

On counter-signature, the contract receives its reference `CONVP-NB-YYYY-XXX` and its sealed PDF, sent to the contact and to the Officers. The partnership becomes **Active**: the partner, solution, nature and programs are then **frozen**.

## Visibility

The partnership's **visibility** stays editable: a presentation (500 characters at most), the partner's website, a logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP, 1 MB at most). A preview is displayed.

An active partnership appears:

- on the page of **each of its programs** on the NextBoxx website;
- in a "**Partners**" block of the Sève course summary of the cohorts concerned, updated every hour;
- for learners, under **Partner tools** in their space.

## The end of a partnership

The partnership **ends the day after its end date**, set at creation or later. It becomes **Ended** and the website no longer shows it.

A cohort of a program concerned **keeps the partner until its own end**, in Sève and in the learners' space, if it has started, or if one of its enrolments was signed, at the latest on the partnership's end date.
