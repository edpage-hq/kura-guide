# L'Éclosion

L'Éclosion is NextBoxx's incubation program. It is open to former **Canopé** learners, to **external project holders** and to **contest laureates**. It runs in **cycles**, with an application deadline.

## Applying

Any account can apply from **Activities → My L'Éclosion project**, **once per cycle**, as long as submissions are open (the page shows the closing date). If no cycle is open, the page says so.

Fill in:

- the **project name** and its **sector**;
- the **problem** it addresses and the **solution** proposed;
- the **progress**: prototype, first customers, team, figures;
- a **pitch** as a PDF, optional (5 MB at most).

The application's **origin** is kept: the "Canopé" route is only offered to an account in the Canopé status; others apply as external project holders. **Contest laureates** are entered for their account by the Incubation Officer.

The Incubation Officer examines your application and informs you of their decision. If your project is **retained**, you become a **Project holder** and a NextBoxx participation may be proposed to you. You follow the application, the decision and the validated participation on the same page. If the project is refused, you receive the reason.

## Running the cycles (Incubation Officer)

The Incubation Officer manages **Activities → L'Éclosion**.

1. **New cycle**: title, **submission deadline**, **start** and **end** of incubation. Submissions close at the latest at the start of incubation. The cycle is created as a **draft**: nobody can apply yet.
2. **Open to applications**: accounts can apply until the deadline.
3. **Enter a laureate**: find the account by its e-mail address, with the contest's name and year. This is possible until the end of incubation.
4. **Examine** each application, with its documents: **Retain the project**, or **Refuse** with a reason. Only the Incubation Officer decides.

## The participation and the investment committee

For a retained project with no participation in progress, the Officer **proposes a participation**: the **type** (equity stake, or revenue share), the **percentage** and the **terms** (duration, exit clause, counterparts). **Submit to the committee** places it "With the committee".

The **investment committee** has three seats: the **Incubation Officer**, the **Pedagogical Coordinator** and the **Direction**. It decides **unanimously**:

- the proposal counts as the Officer's **vote for**;
- the Coordinator and the Direction then vote, **each from a different account** (one person does not sit twice on the same participation);
- a **vote against**, with its reason, **refuses** the participation; the Officer receives the reason and can propose a new one.

Members vote from **Activities → Investment committee**, which lists participations **To vote** and recent decisions. A participation is **only activated once validated** by all three votes. The Direction sees the portfolio of validated participations.
