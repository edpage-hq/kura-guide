# Accounts and roles

The Administrator has access to the whole application. Their **Administration** section gathers the management of accounts, roles and settings.

## Role requests

**Administration → Role requests** is the screen for people who approve roles; the Administrator can approve all of them, in addition to the actor designated for each, so that no request stays blocked by a vacant post.

| Role                                                 | Normally approved by                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Lead Trainer, Coach-Tutor                            | Pedagogical Coordinator                          |
| Admissions Advisor, Officers, Direction, Coordinator | Administrator                                    |
| Business referrer, Marketing partner, Funder         | Partnerships Officer                             |
| Event funder                                         | Events Officer                                   |
| Project holder                                       | Incubation Officer, when the project is retained |

The screen has:

- **Awaiting a decision**: each request with the person's message. **Approve** activates the role at once and notifies the person by e-mail; **Refuse** requires a **reason**, sent by e-mail. A refusal does not touch the person's other roles;
- **Holders**: accounts with a role you approve, with the date and author of the approval, **Withdraw** (with a reason);
- **Grant a role** directly to an existing account, without a prior request.

Nobody decides their **own requests** or withdraws **their own role**.

## Users

**Administration → Users** lists accounts, which you search by name or e-mail, filter by role, and **export as CSV** (respecting the current search). For each: their roles, status, verification state and **last sign-in**.

- **New user** creates an account, for example for a staff member, with their roles. At creation, you assign any role, even one that someone else normally approves. A new account cannot be a Funder, since it is an individual account.
- **Edit** updates the name, e-mail, phone (in international format) and, if you wish, the password; an empty field keeps the current one. Each change is **logged**. **Manage roles** leads to role requests.
- **Delete** removes the account for good. You cannot delete **your own account** from this panel.

### Account statuses

| Status      | Meaning                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active      | Normal account                                                                                                       |
| Suspended   | Non-payment after D+30: no paid action, whatever the module, and no more access to Sève. Payment restores everything |
| Canopé      | Former certified learner                                                                                             |
| Deactivated | Inactive for a year: data is kept and the account reactivates at the next sign-in                                    |

## Organization accounts

**Administration → Organization accounts** lists requests to convert an individual account into an organization account, for **the Administrator alone**.

- **Awaiting a decision**: the account, the company name requested, the **supporting document** (company registration or equivalent, to download), the person's message.
- **Approve**: the account takes the company name as its **name**, the **old name is kept** in the request and in the log, and the person is notified. Their history stays readable, read-only.
- **Refuse**: a **reason** is mandatory, sent by e-mail; the account stays unchanged and can ask again.

You do not decide your own request. An account that already has a request in progress cannot submit a second, and an organization account cannot ask again.
