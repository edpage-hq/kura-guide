# Settings

**Administration → Settings** gathers the application's general parameters. They apply to everything Kura produces afterwards: agreements, invoices, certificates.

## Agreements and legal mentions

- **Counter-signature of agreements**: **Automatic, in the representative's name** (default), or **By the Pedagogical Coordinator**. The same setting applies to the training agreement, the tripartite, the internship agreement and the partnership contract. In manual mode, the Coordinator counter-signs them, or a Partnerships Officer for a partnership.
- **Representative of edPage group**, in whose name automatic counter-signature is made.
- **Registered address**, **RCCM** and **NIF**: printed on documents.

## Invoices

- **VAT mention on invoices**: the exemption mention printed on each invoice (amounts are excluding tax).
- **Payment details (on invoices)**: Mobile Money numbers, bank details and others, printed on each invoice and shown to the learner.
- **Placement fee** and **support fee** (FCFA, per retained profile). **As long as these fees are not set, confirming a placement is impossible.** See [Recruiting certified profiles](../entreprises/recrutement).

## Certificate mentions

The thresholds, as an average out of 20, from which a certificate carries the mention **Assez bien**, **Bien** or **Très bien**. Below the first threshold, the mention is "Passable" (from 10/20). Default values are 12, 14 and 16.

## Profitability zones

The **Viable** and **Comfortable** thresholds of each program category (Career Paths, Job Skills, Technologies & Tools), expressed in confirmed enrolments, between 1 and 15. The Comfortable threshold must be **above** the Viable threshold. Below the Viable threshold, the cohort is Fragile.

A change only touches **upcoming cohorts**: a cohort that has started keeps its start zone, and so do remunerations.

## Public holidays

**Administration → Public holidays**: add a date and its label, or delete it. Admission deadlines are counted in **working days** (Monday to Friday) excluding these days. **Enter movable feasts every year.** Due dates already set keep their date.
