# Welcome

Kura is the administrative and financial platform of NextBoxx, edPage group's training organisation. It accompanies a person from their application to their certificate, and tracks everything administrative along the way: file, agreement, payments, attendance, results.

## Kura and Sève

Two tools share the journey:

|               | Kura                                      | Sève                                                                                    |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Role          | File, agreements, invoices, certificates  | Courses, virtual classes, grades, messaging                                             |
| You use it to | Apply, sign, pay, download your documents | Follow your training                                                                    |
| Sign-in       | NextBoxx account (e-mail and password)    | The same NextBoxx account: Sève relies on Kura to identify you, with no second password |

Your Sève account is opened for you once the first instalment of the training is paid. Kura then passes on to Sève what concerns your access: opening, suspension in case of non-payment, switch to the Canopé state after the certificate. In the other direction, Sève sends back to Kura your attendance at virtual classes and your grades.

## One account, several roles

A person has a single NextBoxx account, identified by their e-mail address. The account accumulates roles over time: candidate, then learner, then Canopé, and perhaps trainer or event funder later. An account is never created twice.

What you see in the menu depends on your roles. It is arranged in six sections:

| Section        | Content                                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overview       | Dashboard, Direction view, reporting                                                                                         |
| Training       | Catalogue, cohorts, profitability, communication                                                                             |
| Journeys       | Candidatures, Enrolments, Company requests, Funded learners, My candidature, My enrolment, My company requests, My documents |
| Activities     | Events, L'Éclosion, Technology partnerships, Investment committee, My events, My funding, My L'Éclosion project              |
| Finance        | Billing, Commissions, Remunerations, Marketing partners, My commissions, My remuneration, My invoices                        |
| Administration | Role requests, Users, Organization accounts, Public holidays, Sève sync, Audit log, Settings                                 |

An entry missing from your menu is not open to you: ask for the matching role (see [Getting started](./premiers-pas#ask-for-a-role)).

## How to read this guide

Each role has its own section, in the left-hand menu. Start with [Getting started](./premiers-pas), common to everyone, then go to your journey.

- **You are applying for a training, or already following yours**: [The learner's journey](./apprenant/candidature).
- **You represent a company** (funding a learner, training your teams, recruiting): [Companies and funders](./entreprises/financeur).
- **You teach, coach or handle applications**: [The pedagogical team](./equipe/formateur).
- **You coordinate the training** (catalogue, cohorts, billing, certification): [Pedagogical Coordinator](./coordinateur/catalogue).
- **You take part in an event, fund, incubate or build a partnership**: [Extended activities](./activites/evenements).
- **You steer or administer the platform**: [Direction and reporting](./direction/pilotage) and [Administrator](./administrateur/comptes-et-roles).
